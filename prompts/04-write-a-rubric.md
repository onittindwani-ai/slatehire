# Write a rubric prompt

> I am hiring for [role]. Interview me about what the job needs, one question at a time. Then write 4 to 6 criteria, each with a plain description, a weight, words that show it in a resume, and short anchors for 1, 3 and 5 that describe work, not people. Flag any criterion that could stand in for a protected trait (a school name, a gap in work, a graduation year). Do not lock it: I will review it in the app.
