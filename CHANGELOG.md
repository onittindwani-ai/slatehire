Round 5 walk of every screen: declining someone closes their waiting offer, deleting personal data also clears the offer text and file, hiring starts the onboarding plan, names and emails are checked, empty files are refused, reveals are logged once, times show in the browser's own clock, read only sign ins see switched off controls, the palette gained sign out, kill switch, theme, run, +1 day and reset, tables open by tap, and failed actions no longer show a success tick.
