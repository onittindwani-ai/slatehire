# Business basics check

Before you hire anyone through any tool, check the basics. The hub repository has a longer guide (`00-BUSINESS-BASICS.md`). The short version for hiring:

1. **A legal entity and an employer account** where you will employ people, with payroll set up or a payroll provider chosen.
2. **The employment rules where the person will work**: contract form, notice, probation, working time, minimum pay. Ask a local adviser. Slatehire writes no offer terms.
3. **Hiring and data rules where you recruit.** Some places regulate automated tools used in hiring, require notice to applicants, or require an audit of bias. Check before you use any scoring tool. This is not legal advice.
4. **A retention rule** for applicant data. Slatehire defaults to 365 days after a decline or withdrawal and lets you change it. Pick yours on purpose.
5. **A privacy notice** to applicants that says what is collected, who reads it, how scoring works and how to ask for deletion.
6. **Who the two people are** who will approve offers. Offers need a second person.

Run `make check-env` to check your environment file.
