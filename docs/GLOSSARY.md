# Glossary

- **Adapter**: a small piece that connects Slatehire to one outside tool.
- **ATS**: applicant tracking system, where a company keeps its job posts and applicants.
- **Anchor**: a plain sentence in a rubric that says what a 1, a 3 and a 5 look like.
- **Audit log**: the list of every change, chained so an edit afterwards shows.
- **Blind mode**: names, emails, schools and similar details are hidden from everyone while a person is at the applied stage.
- **Criterion**: one thing a role is judged on, with a weight.
- **Draft mode**: nothing leaves the app (no invites).
- **Evidence quote**: a word for word piece of the resume that supports a score.
- **Fairness indicator**: a comparison of selection rates between voluntary groups, using the four fifths rule of thumb.
- **Kill switch**: one switch that pauses invites and offer files.
- **Locked rubric**: a rubric that can no longer change. A change makes a new version.
- **Neutral code**: a short code that stands in for a person in the audit log.
- **Redaction**: hiding identity details with uniform placeholders.
- **Retention**: how long a declined person's details are kept.
- **Scorecard**: one interviewer's structured rating, hidden until they send it.
- **Spread**: the gap between two interviewers' ratings. A gap of 2 or more is flagged.
- **Webhook**: a message another tool sends when an application arrives, signed so Slatehire can check it.
